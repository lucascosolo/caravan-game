## The party owner's pay per encounter — ONE rule (chunk 15 fixer, reconciling S4 with chunk 19 QA D1)
Followed as the orchestrator brief stated it; no earlier punch-list decision says otherwise.
- **Held** (the party was wiped): depth-scaled loot = floor(full × d × isqrt(d × 1024) / 1024²), `full` = `coinForEncounter(n, band)` with `band` from the CHOSEN menu row (`chosenEncounterTerms`), exactly the schedule the caravan's `coin_accrued` starts from (chunk 19's sandbagging floor shapes the caravan's earn, not the owner's). Credited at settle as `coin_earned` reason `party_loot` under `transfer:<jobId>`, capped by the owner's headroom (`overflowDiscarded` recorded).
- **Overrun**: chunk 19's transfer of the trip's unbanked Coin + COIN_PER_PARTY_WIN (one capped batch under `transfer:<jobId>`), and loot = 0 — that pay IS the "full clear"; `FULL_CLEAR_LOOT_SHARE` was removed from the tunables (it would pay the same clear twice).
- **Stalemate**: nothing.
- **System (authored) owner**: nothing, ever; the stamp is 0, so the reported loot is 0 too.
- Consistency: `full` is stamped into the job input at enqueue (`partyLootFull`), so the handler's reported `outcome.loot` (job.result, `encounter_resolved`, `party_served_as_encounter`) is a pure function of the job input and equals the credit's requested amount (credited + discarded). The feed adds `lootCoin`. One batch per job under `transfer:<jobId>` (a job is held OR overrun), and the (causation_id, seq) uniqueness makes a second credit impossible.
